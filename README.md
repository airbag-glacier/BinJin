A seamless, user-friendly digital storefront and ordering system built specifically for **BinJin Cookies**. This application streamlines the browsing, cart management, and checkout process, ensuring a smooth end-to-end experience for customers satisfying their cookie cravings.

## 📖 About The Project

Managing orders manually can lead to mix-ups and inefficiencies. The BinJin Cookies Online Ordering System digitalizes the entire workflow. It features a clean, minimalist UI focused on product visibility, allowing users to easily select their favorite cookies, manage their cart, and place orders securely. 

## ✨ Key Features

- **Product Catalog:** A visually appealing showcase of all available BinJin cookie flavors and bundles.
- **Cart Management:** Dynamic shopping cart allowing users to add, remove, and adjust quantities before checkout.
- **Order Tracking:** Automated tracking to keep the customer updated on their order status (Pending, Baking, Out for Delivery).
- **User Authentication:** Secure login and registration for customers to save delivery details and view order history.
- **Admin Dashboard:** A dedicated view for store owners to manage inventory, update product details, and track incoming orders.
- **Clean UI/UX:** Designed with a minimalist, editorial aesthetic for intuitive navigation.
